    Final · Step 2 · Coach's Orders

    Coach's secret code

    Coach Sam has one last lock. It opens with a code that only Coach can say.

    This station is locked

    First open Coach's Door (final step 1) with the letters from stations 1 to 4.

    Go to Coach's Door

    How it works

    1. All players go to Frau Halser together. Take a pencil.
    2. Frau Halser reads Coach's orders. Everybody does the movement, but only when you hear «Coach says». No «Coach says»? It is a trap. Stand still!
    3. After every «Coach says» order, write the first letter of the body part. Keep the letters in order.
    4. Come back and type the four letters here. They make a word.

    Coach's code

    Coach is free! Thank you, team!

    Coach Sam says: “You listened very well! Now it's time to warm up for Sports Day. Do the workout together.”

      Station 4 · Back-to-back Poses

      Find the envelope

      Coach hid the pose cards in an envelope. Solve the riddle to find it. Two players, then back to back.

      Part 1: Coach's riddle

      I have a neck, a body and a head, but I am not a person.
      I have six strings.
      You play me with your fingers.
      What am I?

      Part 2: Build the poses

      Go to the guitar. Next to it you find the envelope. Take it back to your table.

      1. Stand back to back. Person A takes a card and describes the pose in English. Person B builds the pose.
      2. Turn around and compare with the card. (A mirror image is OK!)
      3. Swap roles. Do all four cards.
      4. Then finish the sentences below.

      Coach's question
